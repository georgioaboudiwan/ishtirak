⚡ Ishtirak release
Version 1.7.0
15 November 2026
Here is what changed in this version.
Next step on Home
One card tells you what to do now: set prices, finish readings (and which building to start with), create bills, send them, collect, or follow up late payers.
Door to door
Unpaid people sorted by building and floor with big Paid, Part and Not home buttons. Not home goes to the end of the round. Works for you and your collectors.
Faster meter rounds
Readings are grouped by building with a done count. Enter jumps to the next meter. Readings lower than last month or far above the usual are flagged before saving.
Official price check
Add the Ministry price per kWh and your area (below or above 700 m). The app warns you when your price is above it at your dollar rate.
Late payers and cuts
Everyone 2 months or more behind in one list. Warn them on WhatsApp or by notification, mark them cut, and add a reconnection fee when they pay.
Costs and profit
Log diesel (with liters), oil, repairs and salaries. See your monthly profit, diesel price per liter, and the kWh price you need to break even or make 20%.
Whish and OMT payments
Subscribers tap I paid by Whish / OMT with the amount and transfer number. You confirm with one tap and the bill updates.
Subscribers send their reading
Subscribers can send their meter reading with a photo. You approve it from Home.
Usage chart
Subscribers see their use for the last months and an estimate of their next bill.
Undo
After recording or removing a payment you get a few seconds to undo it, instead of Are you sure? screens.
Search and filters
Search bills by name, phone or building. Filter subscribers by who owes, meter or flat, no link yet, cut, and by building.
PDF receipts
Share a clean PDF receipt on WhatsApp.
Large text and Arabic numbers
More > Text size, and in Arabic More > Numbers for ١٢٣.
Problem reports and app logs
Errors are logged automatically and anyone can report a problem from More. You see everything in More > App logs. Reports are emailed to you at once, errors once an hour.
