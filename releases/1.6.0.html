⚡ Ishtirak release
Version 1.6.0
14 November 2026
Here is what changed in this version.
Notifications on the phone
Subscribers get a notification when their bill is ready, when a payment is recorded, and when you post a notice about a cut or maintenance. You get one when someone asks to join with your code. Turn it on from More > Notifications. On iPhone, add the app to the Home Screen first.
Automatic payment reminders
Subscribers who still owe get a reminder a set number of days after the bill (7 by default), repeated at the same interval until they pay. Change or turn it off from More > Payment reminders.
Meter photos
Tap the camera next to a reading to photograph the meter. The photo is shrunk and encrypted on your phone. You see it on the bill, and the subscriber sees it in their bill details, so there is no argument about the reading.
Import from Excel
Add all your subscribers at once from an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file, with English or Arabic column names. You see a preview, the rows that will be skipped and why, and duplicate phone numbers are caught. Then a queue helps you send everyone their link on WhatsApp.
Template file
Download a ready template from the import screen to fill in.
Photos removed with the subscriber
Deleting a subscriber also deletes their meter photos.
Open Ishtirak

If the old version still shows, close the app and open it again.
