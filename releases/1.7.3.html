⚡ Ishtirak release
Version 1.7.3
16 November 2026
Here is what changed in this version.
Much fewer requests
Opening the app now makes 3 requests to the server instead of 13, and Home loads in a single request. Each one skips the extra access checks the old requests went through, so the app stays quick on a weak connection or a busy server.
No more flashing
Home no longer flashes or jumps back to the top when it updates itself in the background. If nothing changed, it does not redraw at all.
Opens before signing in again
The app shows your last Home at once, even while your sign-in is being renewed in the background.
Open Ishtirak

If the old version still shows, close the app and open it again.
