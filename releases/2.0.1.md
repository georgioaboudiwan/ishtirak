# Ishtirak 2.0.1 (20 November 2026)

- **Real paper receipt:** After a payment you get a proper receipt like the generator's paper slip: your logo and name, the subscriber's details, previous and current reading, consumption, price and fixed fee, the bill total in dollars and lira, what was paid, what is left, a PAID stamp and a signature line. Send it as an image, save it as PDF or print it.
- **See which option you picked:** On the payment screen, “All in $” or “All in LBP” stays highlighted when you tap it, and clears if you type a different amount.
