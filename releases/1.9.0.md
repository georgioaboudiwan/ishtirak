# Ishtirak 1.9.0 (20 November 2026)

- **Read the meter with the camera:** Tap the camera next to a meter and line the numbers up inside the frame. The phone reads the number and fills it in for you, and you can still change it. It works offline, nothing leaves the phone, and it warns you if the number looks lower or much higher than last month. The photo is saved as proof. Subscribers get the same scanner when they send their reading.
- **Say the readings out loud:** On the readings screen, tap “Say the readings” and say each number in Arabic or English. The app fills one meter after the other. Say “next” to skip or “stop” to finish.
- **Generator on and off in one tap:** Home has three buttons: Off now, Power back and Maintenance (with times). Every subscriber with the app is notified at once, and one more tap sends it on WhatsApp to those without it. Subscribers see it at the top of their screen.
- **No power reports, grouped by building:** Subscribers tap “No power at my place”. Reports from the same building are grouped together, which usually means a line or breaker problem there. One tap tells all of them “We're on it”, another tells them “Fixed”.
- **Subscriber requests:** Subscribers can ask to change their amps or send you a short message. You apply an amps change with one tap and they are told it is done.
- **Cash with collectors:** The Collectors screen shows how much cash each collector is holding for you. When they hand it over, tap Received. Collectors see what they have to hand over on their Today screen.
- **Oil and diesel reminders:** Home reminds you when the oil change is due (every 15 days by default, change it in Costs) and when the diesel may run out soon, worked out from your diesel refills.
- **Generator load:** Add your generator size in More > Business. The Subscribers screen shows how much of it is used, and the app warns you before adding someone who would overload it.
- **Pay in dollars and lira together:** The payment screen takes dollars and lira at the same time and shows the change to give back in both. The receipt shows both amounts.
- **Statement and monthly report:** Share a subscriber's full statement (all bills, payments and balance) on WhatsApp or as a PDF. Reports now has a one-page monthly PDF with collections, costs and profit.
