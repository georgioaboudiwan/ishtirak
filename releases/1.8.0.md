# Ishtirak 1.8.0 (16 November 2026)

- **SMS for people without WhatsApp:** Tick "No WhatsApp: send messages by SMS" on a subscriber. Bills, links, reminders and late warnings for them then open your phone's SMS app with the message ready. You can always switch to the other one with one tap.
- **Official price filled in for you:** The Ministry's price per kWh is now looked up automatically every month and filled in on the prices screen. October 2026 is already there (52,622 LBP in cities, 57,884 LBP above 700 m). If the new month isn't published yet, one tap uses the latest one.
- **Faster for subscribers and collectors:** The subscriber's bills screen and the collector's screens now load in a single request, like Home.
