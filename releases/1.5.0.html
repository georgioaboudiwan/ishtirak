⚡ Ishtirak release
Version 1.5.0
14 November 2026
Here is what changed in this version.
Fits every phone
The app opens at the right size and no longer zooms or slides sideways.
Much faster
Opens instantly from the phone after the first visit; switching tabs reuses data already loaded instead of downloading it again.
Ready for Android and iPhone
Store-ready app projects with icons, splash screens and version numbers. Links, sharing and Excel export work inside the app.
Swipe down to close
Every form slides down to close, like a native app.
New design
IBM Plex Sans Arabic font, night-blue colours, an amp-meter bar for what you collected, cleaner lists.
Fixed: the + button
It no longer jumps to the top of the screen when switching tabs.
Professional emails
New bilingual sign-up and password-reset emails with a large, easy-to-read code.
