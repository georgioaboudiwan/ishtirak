⚡ Ishtirak release
Version 2.0.0
20 November 2026
Here is what changed in this version.
A new, simpler Home
One large card tells you the single next thing to do, with a clear button (set prices, start the meters, send the bills, start collecting). A strip under it shows where you are in the month: prices, meters, bills, collection.
Collections on a dial
This month's money shows on a dial like the ammeter on a generator panel, with one sentence under it: how much is left and with how many people.
Big tiles for everything
Meters, collection, the generator (with a live on/off light), late payers, prices and costs are each one big tile on Home. Anything that needs you, like outages, requests or the oil change, shows above them.
A clearer subscriber screen
Subscribers see what they owe in large numbers, one button to tell you they paid by Whish or OMT, and three tiles: send the meter, report a problem, call.
Clear standard Arabic
All Arabic text in the app and in notifications is now in clear standard Arabic, using “المولّد” throughout, with the same digits everywhere.
Create or update bills from Bills
The Bills screen now has a Create bills / Update bills button, so you can redo the bills after late readings.
Open Ishtirak

If the old version still shows, close the app and open it again.
