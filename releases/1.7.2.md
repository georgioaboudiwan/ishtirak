# Ishtirak 1.7.2 (15 November 2026)

- **Opens instantly:** Home now opens straight away from what you saw last time, then updates itself in the background a moment later. On a slow connection with 300 subscribers it went from about 0.9 s to 0.15 s.
- **Faster tabs:** Going back to Home is instant, Bills reuses what Home already loaded, and subscriber details decrypt in parallel.
- **Fewer requests:** Payments and readings to confirm load together with the rest of Home instead of after it.
